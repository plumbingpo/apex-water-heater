Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

A dish room without enough hot water can't sanitize, and inspectors check the handwashing sinks right away. We keep temperatures and recovery where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, verify proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched quickly for emergency repairs anywhere in Apex and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is pinpointing the fault and getting hot water back fast, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. After that, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Apex Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan sized to your property and budget.
